1\. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TruPath AI is a behavioral intelligence platform with a coaching interface.

It is not a chatbot. It is not an AI clone in the conventional sense. It is not a knowledge base with a chat window on top.

It is a system that knows each client's behavioral patterns at a deep structural level, tracks their journey over time, and engages them in conversations that simultaneously coach them and build a richer picture of who they are and how they operate.

Every conversation does three things at once:  
  1\. Coaches the client in real time  
  2\. Fills gaps in their behavioral profile  
  3\. Measures their progress

2\. THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Traditional coaching — and AI coaching tools — have a fundamental limitation: they treat every client conversation as a standalone event. The coach (human or AI) has to re-establish context every time. Pattern identification is shallow. Progress is anecdotal. And the client's access to coaching is constrained by the availability of a human.

Delphi and every comparable platform compounds this: they let practitioners upload a knowledge base, but that knowledge is accessible only through a client-facing chat window. The knowledge cannot be used programmatically. No agent can query it. No coach can search it. It is a brain locked in a box.

TruPath AI solves both problems:  
  — The AI knows each client deeply, across time, not just in the moment  
  — The knowledge layer is a living, queryable asset — not a walled garden

3\. WHY BUILD (NOT BUY)  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

We evaluated every credible platform: Delphi, Personal.ai, Coachvox, Voiceflow, Dust, Relevance AI, and others.

The verdict: build. Not a close call. For three reasons:

REASON 1 — No platform exposes its knowledge base via API  
Every platform we evaluated locks the knowledge behind a proprietary chat interface. There is no way for an agent, a coach dashboard, or an external system to query what the AI knows about a client. This is a structural limitation, not a missing feature. It is how these platforms are designed.

REASON 2 — No platform supports progressive behavioral profiling  
The core capability of TruPath AI — knowing what the client has told us, knowing what we don't yet know, and steering conversations to fill those gaps — requires tight integration between the conversation engine and the client data model. No off-the-shelf platform offers this. It can only be built on top of the FTP-v3 data layer.

REASON 3 — The real-time coaching co-pilot requires a custom build regardless  
Live conversation listening \+ real-time framework overlay is not available on any platform. It requires building. Since we have to build the hardest part anyway, we build everything.

4\. THE CORE PRODUCT INSIGHT  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TruPath AI operates across three time horizons simultaneously:

WITHIN A CONVERSATION  
The Know / Gap / Fill loop. The system loads what it already knows about the client, identifies what is missing from their behavioral profile, and steers the conversation naturally toward filling those gaps. The client experiences coaching. The system does structured data collection underneath.

ACROSS SESSIONS  
Longitudinal progress tracking. After each session, the system computes a behavioral delta against the client's prior state and writes it back to their record. Over time: a time-series behavioral record per client. The AI gets smarter about this person with every exchange.

OVER THE FULL JOURNEY  
Behavioral trajectory. Months and years of session data produce a picture that no single coaching engagement could generate. Pattern evolution, domain-by-domain progress, inflection points. The longer the system runs, the more valuable it becomes.

The progression flow that drives this:

Every conversation opens the same way: "What problem are you facing right now? What friction do you have in your life currently?"

First conversation (no profile yet): The response to that question becomes the entry point into a conversational version of the Tool 1 process — the foundational strategy identification. The client experiences a coaching conversation. The system identifies their dominant behavioral pattern underneath.

Subsequent conversations: The system already knows their pattern. The opening friction question maps to the pattern. The gap analysis identifies which tool dimensions would illuminate this friction. The conversation goes there.

Gap priority logic: friction-driven. The client's stated problem is the navigator. The TruPath framework is the map. The missing tool data is the territory to explore next.

5\. WHO USES IT AND HOW  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Three distinct user types. Three distinct experiences.

THE CLIENT  
Access to Larry's framework and coaching approach 24/7. The AI knows them specifically — their pattern, their history, their progress. Every conversation is personal, not generic. The system meets them where they are and moves them forward.

THE HUMAN COACH  
A co-pilot, not a replacement. The coach sees each client's behavioral profile, progress trajectory, and momentum score before every session. During sessions (Phase 2), the AI listens and surfaces relevant framework insights in real time. After sessions, it logs what happened and updates the client's record.

LARRY  
A thinking partner, content engine, and quality standard. The AI operates in Larry's voice and framework. Larry uses it for content production, teaching material development, and as a research tool against the aggregate behavioral dataset the platform generates over time.

6\. THE MOMENTUM SIGNAL  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every conversation gets ranked: Forward / Neutral / Backwards.

Forward: the client is moving. Patterns are shifting. New insights are integrating. Behavioral signals indicate progress.  
Neutral: maintenance conversation. No measurable movement in either direction.  
Backwards: regression signals detected. The client is reinforcing old patterns, not shifting them.

This ranking is the activation layer for human coaches. The AI handles Forward and Neutral clients autonomously. Backwards surfaces immediately to a coach. That is the human/AI handoff logic — built into the core of the system.

The precise signal vocabulary (what language, behaviors, and patterns map to each tier in TruPath's framework) is a design session with Larry. It is TruPath IP, not something the system invents.

7\. THE IP MOAT  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TruPath AI's defensibility compounds over time. Four layers:

LAYER 1 — Proprietary framework  
The 6 behavioral patterns, 3 domains, 4 life domains, and the intervention logic are TruPath IP. A competitor can build the same architecture. They cannot replicate the framework.

LAYER 2 — Encoded gap analysis logic  
The gap priority-weighting (which dimensions to explore given this pattern \+ this friction \+ this tool history) encodes the framework's internal relationships into the system. This is not documentable without access to the framework. It cannot be reverse-engineered from the outputs.

LAYER 3 — Longitudinal behavioral dataset  
Every conversation adds to a growing dataset of how real people with specific behavioral patterns evolve over time. This dataset has no parallel anywhere. It gets more valuable every day.

LAYER 4 — Momentum scoring requiring framework fluency  
The Forward/Neutral/Backwards scoring requires understanding what progress looks like within TruPath's model of human change. An LLM wrapper cannot score momentum. The framework has to be baked in.

No platform can build this. A competitor who starts building a similar architecture today is starting from zero on all four layers.

8\. PHASED ROLLOUT  
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PHASE 1 — Core engine \+ client chat (2-3 Boris days)  
The foundational system. Client-facing chat powered by TruPath framework knowledge and FTP-v3 profile data. Know/Gap/Fill loop operational. Auto-write back to client record. Momentum scoring basic. Delphi runs in parallel — cancel when quality bar is reached.

PHASE 2 — Coach dashboard \+ longitudinal tracking (1-2 Boris days)  
Coach-facing interface. Client profiles, progress trajectories, momentum scores, Backwards alerts. Longitudinal behavioral delta visible across sessions.

PHASE 3 — Live co-pilot (1-2 Boris days)  
Real-time conversation listening. Streaming transcription → framework insight overlay for coaches during live sessions. Target: 2-3 months post-Phase 1\.

DELPHI EXIT  
Cancel upon Phase 1 reaching quality bar. No data migration needed — FTP-v3 data layer already supersedes Delphi's knowledge base.

PRODUCTIZATION POTENTIAL  
The architecture is not TruPath-exclusive. Any coaching framework with structured assessment data could run on this stack. The platform itself — behavioral intelligence engine \+ progressive profiling \+ longitudinal tracking \+ momentum scoring — is a product that could be licensed or white-labeled. This is a longer-term consideration, not a Phase 1 priority.

CONNECTION TO RAG INFRASTRUCTURE  
The AI twin's knowledge layer (TruPath framework documents, coaching methodology, Larry's voice) is the first deployment of the RAG infrastructure being built in parallel. Access segmentation (CLIENT / COACH / LARRY tiers) is the first real-world test of that system. These two projects are not parallel — they are the same project.

DATA INFRASTRUCTURE EVOLUTION  
Added: 2026-04-08

The original architecture described TruPath AI connecting to FTP-v3 via Google Sheets API — both systems sharing Sheets as the data store. After a full codebase analysis, this approach has a shelf life. TruPath AI will generate significantly more data volume than FTP-v3 (conversation logs, momentum scores, insight writes on every exchange). Google Sheets API rate limits become a constraint at meaningful scale.

The revised architecture introduces a real database (Supabase/Postgres) as the primary data store for TruPath AI. FTP-v3 continues writing to Sheets unchanged. A lightweight sync layer mirrors Sheet data into the database. TruPath AI reads and writes exclusively to the database. Selected data (momentum scores, conversation summaries, coach alerts) syncs back to Sheets so coaches keep their spreadsheet command center.

This changes nothing about the product vision. It changes where TruPath AI stores and queries data — and it opens a long-term path for FTP-v3 to migrate incrementally to the database if and when that becomes valuable, without any rewrite or disruption to the working product.

The database also consolidates the RAG layer: pgvector (Postgres vector extension) means knowledge embeddings, conversation history, and client behavioral data all live in one system. No separate vector database required for Phase 1\.
